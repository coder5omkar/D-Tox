## Ambient sounds

The four bundled ambience loops (in `app/src/main/res/raw/`) are real field
recordings. Each was trimmed to a seamless loop, loudness-normalised and
re-encoded to Ogg Vorbis for D-Tox. The adapted audio files are distributed
in the APK under the same license as their originals, as those licenses
require.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Ocean | "Sea waves.wav" | Ganesh Mohan T | **CC BY-SA 4.0** | [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sea_waves.wav) |
